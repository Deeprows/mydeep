How to install

  1. Download the APK

    Tap Download APK above. If your browser warns that the file may be harmful, choose Download anyway.

  2. Allow installation

    Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser in Settings, then go back.

  3. Tap Install

    If Play Protect shows a notice about an app from outside the Play Store, choose Install anyway to continue.

  4. Open and watch

    Open Deeprowss, find your match or movie and press play.

Need help?

The app won't install

Make sure the APK downloaded completely and that Android allows installation from your browser. Try the alternative link if it keeps failing.

The app won't open

Restart your device, make sure Android is up to date, and reinstall the latest Deeprowss APK.

How do I update Deeprowss?

Download the newest APK from this page and install it over your current version.
