Troubleshooting

The app won't install

Make sure the APK downloaded completely and Android allows installation from your browser.

The app won't open

Restart your device, make sure Android is up to date, and reinstall the latest Deeprowss APK.

Video isn't playing

Check your internet connection, restart the app, and try again.

How do I update Deeprowss?

Download the newest official APK and install it over your current version.
