I'll explain which token or access is needed for the learning workflow, its current cost and what it covers when you contact me. No price is hidden behind a promise of completely free app development.

Will I build a real Android app?

The goal is to learn how to build, test and install a working Android app. Your results depend on your idea, chosen tools and the work involved.
